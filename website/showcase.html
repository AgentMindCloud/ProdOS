A SMALL START. ROOM TO SHAPE IT.

Bring one clip.
Ask one question.

We’re aiming for 5–10 producers in the first group. You don’t need the desktop app or a website account to take part.

Email your clip link ↗

Opens a draft in your email app. Review it and send it yourself. If no draft opens, email contact@prodos.tech with the details alongside.

Include these details

  1. Your producer name and track title.
  2. A public link to a 30–60 second clip on SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Spotify or YouTube. Send a link, not an attachment.
  3. One specific feedback question. Genre and BPM are optional.
  4. Your explicit permission to feature the link, name, title and question here, and confirmation that you have the necessary rights and collaborator permissions.

Submissions are reviewed before listing. Your email address stays out of the public listing. Inclusion isn’t guaranteed; a listing doesn’t transfer music ownership or grant permission to download, sample or reuse it.

MAKE THE NEXT LISTEN COUNT

Useful feedback.
Human decisions.

Be specific. Be constructive.

Answer the producer’s question. Point to a moment in the clip, explain what you heard, and offer one thing they could try. No spam, personal attacks or promotional links.

Keep control of your listing.

For a correction, removal from the showcase or a rights concern, email the page link and details. We can change or unlist the entry here; your original music stays on its hosting platform.

Request a correction or report a concern ↗

This website collects no submissions automatically and adds no analytics. Details you choose to email are used to review and manage your entry; don’t include private project files or passwords.
